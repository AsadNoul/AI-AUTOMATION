About AI Automation

We're on a mission to democratize intelligent workflow automation and make AI accessible to businesses of all sizes.

Our Mission

Empowering businesses with intelligent automation

AI Automation was born from the belief that every business, regardless of size, should have access to powerful automation tools that leverage artificial intelligence. We combine the flexibility of n8n with the intelligence of modern AI models to create a platform that's both powerful and accessible.

Our goal is to help businesses reduce manual work, eliminate repetitive tasks, and focus on what truly matters - growing their business and serving their customers better.
